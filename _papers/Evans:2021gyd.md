abstract: |
  This Horizon Study describes a next-generation ground-based
  gravitational-wave observatory: Cosmic Explorer. With ten times the
  sensitivity of Advanced LIGO, Cosmic Explorer will push
  gravitational-wave astronomy towards the edge of the observable
  universe ($z \sim 100$). The goals of this Horizon Study are to
  describe and evaluate design concepts for Cosmic Explorer; to plan
  for the United States' leadership in gravitational-wave astronomy;
  and to envisage the role of Cosmic Explorer in the international
  effort to build a "Third-Generation" (3G) observatory network that
  will make discoveries transformative across astronomy, physics, and
  cosmology.
